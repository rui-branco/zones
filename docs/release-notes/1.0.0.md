The first release of Zones: snap windows into zones you draw yourself, on Windows and macOS.

![Dragging a window on an ultrawide, with the middle zone lit](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rui-branco/zones/main/docs/overlay.png)

Hold Shift while you drag a window and the zones of the display under the cursor appear. Let go over one and the window fills it.

- **Your own zones, per display.** Start from one of eight ready-made layouts or draw your own: click a zone to split it, drag the line between two to resize them. Every display keeps its own.
- **Span two zones** by letting go near the line between them. Drag a window out again and it gets its own size back.
- **Sizes.** Keep several versions of a display's layout, the middle at 50%, 60% and 70% say, and flip between them with a shortcut, or with the mouse wheel before you drop. Every window snapped on that display follows its zone.
- **Four shortcuts**: the active window to its next or previous zone (Ctrl+Alt+Z, with Shift to go back), the display to its next or previous size (Ctrl+Alt+X, with Shift). ⌃⌥Z and ⌃⌥X on the Mac. All four can be set to any keys.
- **A settings window** that draws each display's layout to its real proportions, with the ready-made layouts, the sizes, the gap between zones and the shortcuts in one place. Every change is saved as you make it.
- **Windows and macOS**, native on both: C# and WinForms in the tray, Swift and AppKit in the menu bar. Light or dark, and your accent colour.
- **Quiet updates.** From here on, Zones checks for new releases, downloads and checks them in the background, and installs them while you are not using it, back in a second. Turn off **Install updates automatically** in the menu to choose the moment yourself.

On Windows, keep `zones.exe` somewhere permanent such as `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Zones`; it is not code-signed, so SmartScreen may ask first (**More info**, then **Run anyway**). On macOS, move Zones.app to Applications, open it the first time with right-click → **Open** (on macOS 15 and later, **Open Anyway** under Privacy & Security), and turn it on under Privacy & Security → Accessibility.

Download `zones.exe` (Windows) or `Zones-mac.zip` (macOS) below.
