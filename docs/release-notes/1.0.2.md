The settings window has a menu down the left now, one page for each thing it sets, instead of everything on one crowded screen.

- **Layouts**: a tab for each display across the top, and under it the presets, the sizes and the zone editor, which is wider than before.
- **Snapping**: snapping on or off, Hold Shift to snap and the spacing, each with a line saying what it does.
- **Shortcuts**: next and previous zone, next and previous size, each with what it does, and a warning under them if one is taken by another program.
- **General**: Start with Windows (**Start at Login** on macOS), Auto-update, and the version with a **Check for updates** button.
- **The version is always in sight** at the foot of the menu, with an **Update to …** button above it as soon as a newer Zones is out.
- The title bar is back to just the name and the window buttons, and the tray and menu bar item that opens the window is now called **Settings…**.

Zones 1.0.0 and 1.0.1 update themselves to this one. Otherwise download `zones.exe` (Windows) or `Zones-mac.zip` (macOS) below.
