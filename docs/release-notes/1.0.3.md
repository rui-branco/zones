A fix for the settings window on Windows.

- **No more old-style title bar over the window.** Switching to another app could leave a pale blue Windows 95-style title bar, with a "?" button, painted across the top right of the settings window until something redrew it. Windows was drawing its own caption over the window's header whenever it went to the background; it no longer does.

Zones updates itself to this one. Otherwise download `zones.exe` (Windows) or `Zones-mac.zip` (macOS) below.
