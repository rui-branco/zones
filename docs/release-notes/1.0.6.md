On Windows, part of a layout can now be left empty, so a zone can sit in the middle of the display with space around it.

- **Leave part of a layout empty.** Right-click a zone in the editor and pick **Leave empty**. The space keeps its place and the lines beside it still drag, but it takes no number, no window snaps into it, the zone shortcuts skip it and the overlay leaves it out. Cut a zone across twice with Shift+click, empty the top and the bottom, and the one left between them is a zone in the middle of the screen, as tall as you drag it. **Make it a zone** fills an empty part again.
- **The editor says how to cut across.** Its hint now mentions Shift+click, which cuts a zone into a top and a bottom part. It was only in the README before.

Nothing changes on the Mac in this one.

Zones updates itself to this one. Otherwise download `zones.exe` (Windows) or `Zones-mac.zip` (macOS) below.
