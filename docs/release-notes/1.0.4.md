The zone shortcuts now move whatever window is in front, Zones' own included.

- **The settings window snaps too.** With the Zones window in front, the next and previous zone shortcuts used to do nothing at all, on purpose, which only looked like the shortcuts were broken. They now move it into a zone like any other window.
- **Windows that can't shrink stay on screen.** A window with a minimum size bigger than its zone used to hang off the edge of the display when snapped into a narrow zone at the right or bottom. It is now pulled back so all of it shows, as far into the zone as it fits.

Zones updates itself to this one. Otherwise download `zones.exe` (Windows) or `Zones-mac.zip` (macOS) below.
