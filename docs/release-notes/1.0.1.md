The settings window now resizes, and the things you look for first are in plain sight.

- **Resize the window** from any edge or corner. The zone editor grows with it.
- **Version, updates and startup in the title bar.** The running version, a **Check for updates** button that turns into **Update to …** when there is one, and switches for **Auto-update** and **Start with Windows** (**Start at Login** on macOS). They used to be only in the tray and menu bar menus.
- **Reset asks first.** "Reset this display" now asks before it throws away a display's layout and all its sizes.
- **Settings are never quietly dropped.** A part of the settings file Zones can't read is kept and written back as it was, instead of being lost on the next save.
- **Lighter in the background.** Zones hands memory back after the settings window closes, and no longer keeps a thread waiting just to hear from a second launch.

Zones 1.0.0 updates itself to this one. Otherwise download `zones.exe` (Windows) or `Zones-mac.zip` (macOS) below.
